About the day

Campus Care Day is now in its second year. It is a full day of small, useful jobs: sweeping paths, planting seedlings along the main walkway and reading aloud to young readers from a nearby school.

No experience is needed. You can join for the whole day or just one shift, and every crew has a leader who explains the job before you start.

The day, hour by hour

  1. 8:30 AM

    Check in with tea at Sunset Lawn

  2. 9:00 AM

    Welcome and safety briefing in Main Hall

  3. 9:30 AM

    Crews head out: clean-up, garden and reading buddies

  4. 12:30 PM

  5. 1:30 PM

    Second shift

  6. 4:00 PM

Get ready

Two short sessions help you feel prepared. Both are optional.

  • Care Day Info Session

    Wednesday 4 November 2026, 1:00 PM to 1:45 PM, Main Hall, Room 2

    Meet the crew leaders and ask anything before the day.

  • Reading Buddies Training

    Monday 9 November 2026, 4:00 PM to 5:30 PM, Library Seminar Room

    An hour of practice reading aloud. Please come if you choose the reading buddies crew.

What to bring

  • A refillable water bottle
  • Comfortable closed shoes
  • Your student ID, for check-in
  • A hat or sunscreen, because the lawn has little shade
  • Work gloves if you have them. We have spares.
