What a Care Day feels like

Show up

Check in at 8:30 with your student ID, grab a tea and meet your crew. No experience needed, and nobody is graded.

Learn

Each crew leader explains the why before the how: how a seedling grows, where sorted waste goes, how to read aloud to a child.

Pass it on

The day ends with a closing circle at golden hour. Share one thing you learned, then bring a friend to the next one.
